# Energy Trading Between Households

## Project Description
This project is a simple smart contract that facilitates decentralized peer-to-peer energy trading between households. Each household can deposit energy (in kWh) and Ether into their account and trade energy with other users on the network in a trustless, automated way.

## Project Vision
The goal is to empower households to independently trade surplus energy, such as solar power, within a community. By leveraging blockchain, we aim to remove the need for centralized intermediaries and reduce transaction friction, improving energy efficiency and autonomy.

## Key Features
- 🌞 **Energy Deposit**: Users can deposit surplus energy into their account.
- 💸 **P2P Energy Trading**: Households can trade energy directly with each other using Ether as payment.
- 🔎 **Transparent Balances**: Users can view their current energy and Ether balances at any time.
- 🔐 **Secure Transactions**: All transactions are logged on the blockchain for transparency and security.

## Future Scope
- 🔄 **Dynamic Pricing via Auctions or Market Mechanisms**
- 🌍 **Integration with Smart Meters and IoT Devices**
- 📊 **Analytics Dashboard for Energy Usage**
- 🏦 **Incentive Models and Carbon Credit Integration**
- 🔗 **Cross-Chain Interoperability with Green Energy Platforms**
